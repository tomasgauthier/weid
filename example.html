Yesterday I saw a poster for a band with three sold-out nights. The dates: March 2027. It is August 2026. Which is to say, thousands of people have already paid to be together, in a specific place, seven months from now. I stood looking at that poster longer than I would like to admit, and I was left with a single question: what makes that possible?

The Shenzhen–Maipú image and the phrase "miracle of coordination" are the model's.Trust. It is that simple. Because the system we live in, with all its obvious problems — and they are serious, starting with how wealth concentrates —, rests on a foundation we almost never look at head-on: it lets us predict future behaviour with enough certainty to act today. A parcel crossing the Pacific from a warehouse in Shenzhen to a door in Maipú, without anyone in the chain knowing anyone else, is a miracle of coordination we stopped marvelling at out of sheer excess of functioning.

The correction — variety as mechanism and not as excess — is the argument the model set against my original thesis.Variety is not the side effect of the system that works: it is the mechanism by which it works. AliExpress delivers because, if it doesn't, there is the other one. The twenty car brands are the reason each one works at not failing you.

That differentiation, luxury and even overconsumption should run in the digital, where the material cost per unit of status is orders of magnitude lower. A digital cosmetic is a server plus a screen; a premium pickup truck is mining, manufacturing, freight and, sooner or later, scrap.
